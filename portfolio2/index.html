Improving an India GDP Growth Visualization

Redesigning a visualization of India's real GDP per capita growth.

Original Visualization

The original chart resets each prime minister's term to an index of 100, which makes periods of different lengths appear directly comparable.

Why I Chose This Visualization

I chose this visualization because I liked the idea of comparing India's economic growth over time, but the original chart made the comparison more confusing than it needed to be. Each prime minister's period starts again at an index value of 100, even though the periods are different lengths. This makes the lines look easy to compare at first, but they do not actually show how fast GDP per capita was growing each year.

Improved Visualization

What I Changed

I redesigned the visualization using annual GDP per capita growth from the World Bank. Instead of restarting the data at 100 for each period, every year now uses the same measurement and the same time scale. I also added a zero-growth reference line and highlighted 2020, which stands out as the largest decline in the chart. I considered keeping the indexed format from the original, but annual percentage growth makes changes from year to year much easier to compare.

The improved chart makes it easier to see periods of strong growth, slowdowns, and economic decline without having to compare separate indexed lines.
